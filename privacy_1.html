Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026 · Operated by Tachyon Software Engineering & KI Tool Development AG, Switzerland

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Tachyon Software Engineering & KI Tool Development AG ("we", "us") processes personal data in connection with our content publishing application (the "Service"). We process personal data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What the Service Does

The Service is an internal content publishing tool used to schedule and publish media content to connected third-party platforms, including TikTok, via their official APIs. It is operated exclusively for the account owner's own content. The Service does not offer public user registration and does not collect personal data from the general public.

3. Data We Process

To provide the Service, we process the following limited categories of data:

  • Platform account data: access tokens and basic account identifiers of the connected TikTok account, provided through TikTok's official OAuth authorization flow;
  • Content data: media files, captions, and publishing metadata created by the account owner for publication;
  • Technical logs: API request logs required for operating, debugging, and securing the Service.

We do not collect data about viewers, followers, or other third-party users of connected platforms beyond what the platform's API returns for standard publishing operations.

4. Purpose and Legal Basis

Data is processed solely to operate the Service: authenticating with connected platforms, publishing content on behalf of the account owner, and maintaining the security and reliability of the system. The legal basis is the performance of the service relationship and our legitimate interest in operating a secure system.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with:

  • the connected platforms (e.g., TikTok) as required to publish content via their APIs;
  • technical infrastructure providers (hosting, storage) bound by data processing agreements.

6. Data Retention

Access tokens are stored only as long as the platform connection is active and are deleted when the connection is revoked. Technical logs are retained for a limited period necessary for security and troubleshooting, and are then deleted.

7. Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data, including encrypted storage of access credentials, access restrictions, and secure transmission (TLS) for all API communication.

8. Your Rights

Data subjects have the right to request information about, correction of, or deletion of their personal data, and to object to processing, subject to applicable law. Requests can be sent to the contact address below.

9. Revoking Platform Access

Access granted to the Service via TikTok can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under account settings ("Security & permissions" → "Apps and websites"). Upon revocation, the associated access tokens become invalid and are removed from our systems.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always published at this URL.
